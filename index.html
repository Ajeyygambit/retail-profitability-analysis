Retail performance & profitability

Where does four years of retail profit actually come from — and where does it leak?

9,994 order lines, 5,009 orders, 2015–2018. The published file ships with two extra blocks appended after the data: a four-row people list and an 800-row returns list. A naive import breaks on the first and silently loses the second. Both are handled here, and the returns block turned out to matter.

What is in the repository

sql/01_clean.sql splits the junk blocks into orders and returns. sql/02_analysis.sql answers the four business questions. dashboard/ is a self-contained interactive page (no server). memo.md is the write-up a manager would read. powerbi/ holds the star-schema spec and DAX pack; excel/ the Power Query recipe.

Data: Tableau Sample Superstore public dataset, synthetic US retail. Not company data — the cleaning, analysis and recommendations are mine. Every figure traces to a query in sql/. Analysis and build by Azam Azhan.
